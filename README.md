PART 1: Building the foundation

Organization 1: Overview
Name: Katie’s kitty café
Background: My client, Katie, runs a small cat café in Woodstock, Cape Town with her family. It was started in hopes of giving stray and abandoned cats a new place to call home, as well as serving delicious treats to feline loving visitors. Since they started, their small café grew into a popular hot spot for locals. They went from a few cats to almost 20, and they are still growing. 
Mission: To raise awareness and funds for stray and abandoned cats across the city.
Target audience: Pet lovers and café enthusiasts
Website goals and objectives
Though they are popular amongst locals, my client wants to branch out to tourists that come and visit cape town during the summer holidays, and they reached out to me in hopes that I can create a website that can attract and inform new and prospective customers remotely, informing them about menu changes and all the cats that inhabit the café.
My client wants their website to match the theme of the café in terms of colour and aesthetic, with a way to show off their main attraction, being the cats. They asked for a welcoming homepage that isn’t too overwhelming, using the colour scheme they provided. 
Proposed web features and functionality
The website should have a warm and inviting environment, and colors are one of the most important ways of implementing that. For the color scheme, I suggest pastel colors, which are soft and cozy, perfect for content creators who want the aesthetic café feel. 
Color theme:
•	Soft pink
•	Sky blue
•	Light yellow
•	Pale mint

I suggested that they have 5 pages: 
•	Homepage: a simple but striking way to showcase the café, equipped with a slideshow with pictures and videos of the café.
•	Meet the cats: a way to present the cats that are part of the café using a slideshow function, allowing them to each have their own spotlight.
•	Menu page: Where they can present and update the café’s menu.
•	About us: A brief history of the café and why it was opened, showcasing the client and their employees.
•	Contact and reviews: Where the café address and socials are, as well as a space for people to leave reviews about their experience and time spent, equipped with a google map of the website and social media handles, as well as a star rating function.
